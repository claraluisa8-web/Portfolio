Belonging in the Gaming and Esports Lab

NC State University Libraries · IRB-approved study

Fall 2025
  • Conducted eight fly-on-the-wall observations for an IRB-approved study, then analyzed the full set of 21 observation logs alongside a 20-response student survey.
  • Found the largest barrier to using the lab was social rather than logistical: 35% of respondents cited having no one to go with, ahead of the 26% who cited getting to the building.
  • Established that respondents averaged ten years of play and that 65% had experienced harassment in gaming, which reframes belonging as a question of social access rather than a skills gap.
  • Showed that only five of the 36 games logged appeared both at programmed events and in general lab use, so the two populations were sharing a room without sharing a community.
  • Renamed the program Femme & Friends Game Night after a participant reported that “femme” read as excluding butch women and transmasc people, and had declined merchandise carrying the same wording.
